# Credits

- The creativity prompt (`mbc2.md`, enabled by `--creative`) is inspired by Margaret Boden's notion of Exploratory Creativity, as described in *The Creative Mind: Myths and Mechanisms*.
- The `scholar` advice (`malvin --advice scholar`) is adapted from *How to ML Paper* by Jakob N. Foerster.
- The `kpop` advice (`malvin --advice kpop`) uses Karl Popper's account of an empirical hypothesis in *The Logic of Scientific Discovery* (Hutchinson, 1959; German edition, 1934): the hypothesis is empirical when some possible observation would count against it.
- The `kpop` advice also uses Michael Timothy Bennett, *The Optimal Choice of Hypothesis Is the Weakest, Not the Shortest* (arXiv:2301.12987, 2023): when tasks are uniformly distributed, the weakest hypothesis that still fits the observations maximizes the probability of generalization.
