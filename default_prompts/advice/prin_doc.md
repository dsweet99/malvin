# Five Principles of Document Design

1. **Design for the Reader:** Make important information easy to understand, find, and remember.
2. **Make Structure Visible:** Use visual hierarchy, contrast, alignment, and consistency to communicate relationships.
3. **Make Text Readable:** Choose typography, spacing, and line lengths for comfortable, sustained reading.
4. **Compose Whole Pages:** Balance content, whitespace, and visual elements to create coherent, attractive pages.
5. **Refine Systematically:** Use reusable styles, inspect rendered pages, and iterate toward better design.


## Notes
- TeX's cleverness can help you get uniformity with less effort. Look for "programmatic" TeX solitions instead of making lots of little tweaks and nudges.
