# Principles of Scientific Visualization

1. **Purpose:** State (for yourself) the intended message before choosing how to visualize the data.
2. **Comparison:** Align related quantities on common scales; label which direction of "better".
3. **Insight:** Organize data to reveal trends, dependencies, variability, uncertainty, deviations from prediction, etc.
4. **Simplicity:** Give every visual element a clear informational or explanatory purpose.
5. **Interpretation:** Plainly state and explain the meaning and significance of the figure in the caption.

Important: **Inspect the actual rendered figure rather than judging only from the source code**. During development, convert or render the figure to a PNG and look at it. Consider
 a human's perspective (visual system, cognitive abilities) as you view the PNG.
